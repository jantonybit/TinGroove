# Privacy Policy — TinGroove

**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** November 24, 2026  
**Publisher:** Jantonybit · **Store ID:** `9N8BSV9BGP95` (confirm in Partner Center)

---

## Summary

TinGroove is a drum sequencer that runs **entirely on your computer**. It creates no account,
collects no personal data, sends no telemetry, displays no ads, and never records or uploads
audio. All bundled sounds are generated by the app itself.

## 1. Who is responsible

TinGroove is published by **Jantonybit**. This policy explains what happens to your files and
information when you use the app. Purchase details are handled by the Microsoft Store, not by
the application.

## 2. Data we collect

**None.** There is no sign-in, analytics, crash reporting, advertising ID, cookie, third-party
SDK, or upload of files, text or audio to any server. The app has no in-app purchases: payment
is a one-time purchase processed by the Store.

## 3. Audio and devices

| Access | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| WASAPI output (render-only, shared mode) | Play the pattern you built | Only to the output device you selected |
| MIDI input (`winmm`) | Receive notes, CC and MIDI Clock from a controller or DAW | Local commands only; nothing is sent over the network |

TinGroove does **not** access the microphone, does not perform loopback capture, does not record
other applications' audio and does not record system audio. It installs no driver of its own.

## 4. Files on your computer

Unlike a classic application, TinGroove **never writes inside the executable's folder** (which
is read-only in the Store package). Everything it saves is kept in:

```
%LOCALAPPDATA%\TinGroove\config\
```

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `ui.cfg` | Language, output device, MIDI preferences, sound engine and stems option |
| `rhythm.cfg` | Your pattern, bars, time signature, loop, durations, mix and mute/solo |
| `samples.cfg` | Instrument, velocity layer and path of each WAV you imported |
| `ritmo.mid` | Last MIDI export |
| `ritmo.wav` and `stems\*.wav` | Last audio export (when you click EXPORT WAV) |

To remove your data, close the app and delete that folder manually.

The app reads **only the WAV file you explicitly pick** in the sample manager. The file is read
in place: it is never copied, modified, renamed or uploaded. If you delete or move it, that slot
falls back to the app-generated sound.

> Use only sounds you have the right to use. Do not redistribute third-party samples or
> recorded keyboard timbres. TinGroove does not bundle or host such files.

## 5. Internet

TinGroove makes no network requests on its own. The only internet-related action is opening,
after an **explicit click** on *More Apps From The Developer.*, the publisher's Microsoft Store
page (with an `apps.microsoft.com` fallback). Your settings and exports are never uploaded.

## 6. Sharing with third parties

We share no data with third parties. There are no ad networks, analytics, cloud services or
payment processors inside the app. Microsoft may process information related to your Microsoft
account and purchase under the Microsoft Store policies.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect information from children.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new
date at the top.

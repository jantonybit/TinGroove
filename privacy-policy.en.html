TinGroove

SOUND DESIGNER BY JANTONYBIT · PRIVACY POLICY

Summary: TinGroove runs entirely on your computer. It creates no account, collects no data, sends no telemetry, displays no ads, and never records or uploads audio. All bundled sounds are generated by the app itself.

1. Who is responsible

TinGroove is published by Jantonybit. This policy explains what happens to your files and information when you use the app. Purchase details are handled by the Microsoft Store, not by the application.

2. Data we collect

None. There is no sign-in, analytics, crash reporting, advertising ID, cookie, third-party SDK, or upload of files, text or audio to any server. The app has no in-app purchases: payment is a one-time purchase processed by the Store.

3. Audio and devices

AccessPurposeWhere it goes
WASAPI output (render-only, shared mode)Play the pattern you builtOnly to the output device you selected
MIDI input (winmm)Receive notes, CC and MIDI Clock from a controller or DAWLocal commands only; nothing is sent over the network

TinGroove does not access the microphone, does not perform loopback capture, does not record other applications' audio and does not record system audio. It installs no driver of its own.

4. Files on your computer

TinGroove never writes inside the executable's folder (which is read-only in the Store package). Everything it saves is kept in:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\TinGroove\config\
FileContents
ui.cfgLanguage, output device, MIDI preferences, sound engine and stems option
rhythm.cfgYour pattern, bars, time signature, loop, durations, mix and mute/solo
samples.cfgInstrument, velocity layer and path of each WAV you imported
ritmo.midLast MIDI export
ritmo.wav and stems\*.wavLast audio export

To remove your data, close the app and delete that folder manually.

The app reads only the WAV file you explicitly pick in the sample manager. The file is read in place: it is never copied, modified, renamed or uploaded. If you delete or move it, that slot falls back to the app-generated sound.

Use only sounds you have the right to use. Do not redistribute third-party samples or recorded keyboard timbres. TinGroove does not bundle or host such files.

5. Internet

TinGroove makes no network requests on its own. The only internet-related action is opening, after an explicit click on More Apps From The Developer., the publisher's Microsoft Store page (with an apps.microsoft.com fallback). Your settings and exports are never uploaded.

6. Sharing with third parties

We share no data with third parties. There are no ad networks, analytics, cloud services or payment processors inside the app. Microsoft may process information related to your Microsoft account and purchase under the Microsoft Store policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect information from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date at the top.

9. Contact

Privacy questions should be filed as an Issue in the TinGroove GitHub repository: github.com/Jantonybit/TinGroove/issues (address to confirm after the repository is created).
